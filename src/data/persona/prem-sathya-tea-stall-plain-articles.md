## Binary multiply — faster way

Normal binary multiply takes time. We found a better way.  

It comes from old Indian math — Nikhilam Sutra.  
Find the base. Subtract. Multiply the small parts. Add back.  

In binary the base is power of two. Many zeros make it quick.  

We wrote code that splits the numbers. Adds the easy part. Recurses on the hard part.  

It beats standard methods when the bits have long strings of zero.  

We tested it. It worked.

## A real problem — seven sites, seven judges

Seven tourist sites. Seven judges.  

Rule: three judges per site.  
Rule: three sites per judge.  
Rule: every pair of sites shares exactly one judge.  

It sounds simple. Try it on paper. You will see it is a puzzle.  

This is constraint programming.  
The computer removes impossible choices fast. It searches the rest in parallel.  

Real life has many such puzzles. Small ones we solve by hand. Big ones need smart code.  

Sudoku is the same game.

## Ergonomics — do not fight your own body

Bala stared at his new phone. His neck hurt.  

The TV showed the same story. Wrong posture. Long hours. Repetitive strain.  

Many people end up in surgery.  

The fix is simple.  
Stand up. Stretch. Change position. Take real breaks.  

I tried it. Work finished faster. Pain left.  

You say you have no time. That means you chose not to care for yourself.  

Your body is the only machine you cannot replace. Treat it well.
