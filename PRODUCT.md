# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

1. **Primary (this wave):** SpaceXAI / xAI–adjacent hiring reviewers and technical interviewers who open `/qa` to pressure-test depth, honesty, and systems taste in minutes.
2. **Secondary:** Senior engineering recruiters and collaborators who want a live, first-person narrative grounded in real work—not a static resume dump.
3. **Tertiary:** The operator (Peramanathan) validating that answers stay accurate after data or model changes.

Context: short attention, high skepticism, comparison against many candidates. They need signal fast: thesis → production ownership → agentic systems judgment → restraint.

## Product Purpose

**devprofile** is a living portfolio for Peramanathan Sathyamoorthy. The **Profile Q&A** surface (`/qa`) is the product moment: ask interview-grade questions and get first-person answers grounded in curated persona sources (golden Q&A, CV, Collections when enabled).

Success looks like:

- A SpaceXAI-caliber reviewer thinks “this person has taste, receipts, and restraint.”
- Answers are **accurate** (grounded in retrieval/golden), **concise**, and **first-person**—not corporate paste.
- Suggested questions showcase the best arcs (2016 EEaaS thesis, Oneflow compound impact, open-source leverage, agentic personal systems) without gimmicks.
- UI feels inevitable: calm confidence, editorial hierarchy, zero SaaS-dashboard cliché.

## Brand Personality

**Quiet leverage · Patient vision · Lived craft**

- Warm senior engineer, not a personal brand agency.
- Dry wit, Orwellian brevity, proof over adjectives.
- Family-aware builder energy without oversharing.
- Emotional goal: *respect + curiosity* — “I want the next answer.”

## Anti-references

- Generic AI portfolio chatbots with purple gradients and “Ask me anything ✨” empty hype.
- SaaS metric hero cards (big numbers + tiny labels + identical icon grids).
- Corporate “About me” voice, LinkedIn-ese, or motivational poster tone.
- Glassmorphism / neon cyberpunk “AI tool” skins.
- Infinite chip walls of weak questions (“What is React?”).

## Design Principles

1. **Show receipts, not claims** — every impressive line should be answerable from sources; UI makes evidence glanceable.
2. **Interview as product** — chips and empty state train the visitor toward high-signal questions SpaceXAI would actually ask.
3. **Editorial, not dashboard** — type hierarchy and whitespace carry prestige; cards only when they hold evidence.
4. **Restraint is the flex** — short answers, limited chips, one clear primary action.
5. **Honest scope** — personal/OSS agent work ≠ multi-year AI-lab employment; never blur that line in UI or copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast on body and interactive text (existing brand tokens).
- Keyboard: form, chips, and answer region fully operable; visible focus rings.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no required motion; entrance fades optional and suppressible.
- Screen readers: real labels, not icon-only controls; status updates announced when loading/errors.
- Placeholder and muted text must stay ≥4.5:1 (no pale gray on warm surfaces).
